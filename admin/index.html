    Add a photo
    
          
    Your own institutional photos only. The photo is cropped to 3:2, resized to 1600 and 800 px and redrawn as new WebP files here in the browser. Everything else in the original file (camera, date, location, author, software) is left behind; below the photo you see what was found and removed, and the new files are checked before they are saved.
    
          
          
          
    
          
    
          
          
          
          
    
          
    New photos show in the Design preview straight away. They reach the public site only when you press "Publish images".
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Which photo goes with which vote
    
          
    "Automatic" uses the library in turn. Votes without any photo show a neutral placeholder.

    Parliament's final vote for each proposal
    
        
    The weekly data job links each proposal to its National Council final vote by itself (same business number, and vote counts that agree with Swissvotes); nothing needs to be set here. A link chosen below overrides it — only for the rare case the job can't link. For an initiative, Parliament votes on a federal decree that sets out its recommendation; the site counts that vote for / against the initiative.
