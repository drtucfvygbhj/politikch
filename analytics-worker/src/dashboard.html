How counting works. No cookies are used, so a visitor is recognised only within one day (Europe/Zurich). Someone who visits on three days counts as three visitors in longer ranges. A visit ends after 30 minutes without a new page. A bounce is a visit with a single page view. Time figures only count time the page was visible.

Finished days are summarised every hour; today is computed live. Raw records are deleted after the retention period set in the Worker, and the daily summaries are kept.
